Prime Rentals — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Prime Rentals ("we," "our," "the app") provides a car rental platform connecting customers with a vehicle rental business, including booking, driver assignment, and related services. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

Information We Collect

  • Account Information: your name, phone number, and email address when you create an account.
  • Booking Information: pickup and drop-off locations (entered as text or detected via your device's approximate location when you choose to use that feature), dates, times, and payment method selected for each booking.
  • Chat Messages: messages you exchange with our admin/support team within the app.
  • Reviews and Ratings: ratings and comments you submit about cars or drivers you have booked.
  • Photos: if you are an admin, photos of vehicles, drivers, and maintenance receipts you upload for business record-keeping. Customer accounts do not upload personal photos.
  • Push Notification Token: a device identifier used solely to deliver notifications about your bookings and messages (Android only).

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and process your bookings.
  • To communicate with you about booking status, driver assignment, and support requests.
  • To send push notifications about booking approvals, rejections, and new messages.
  • To generate handover documentation (pickup/drop-off slips) for approved bookings.
  • To assign a driver to your booking, when requested.
  • To improve our fleet, services, and business operations based on booking history and reviews.

Payments

Prime Rentals does not process, store, or have access to any payment card, bank account, or mobile wallet credentials. For online payments, we display our business account details (e.g. JazzCash, EasyPaisa, or bank transfer information) and you send payment directly through your own banking or mobile wallet app. Our team manually verifies receipt of payment; we never collect your payment account passwords or PINs.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share limited information only as follows:

  • With a driver assigned to your booking (name and phone number only, for coordination purposes).
  • With our cloud service providers (Supabase for data storage and authentication, Google Firebase for push notification delivery) strictly to operate the app — these providers do not use your data for their own purposes.
  • As required by applicable law.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a cloud database provider, with encrypted connections and access controls that restrict data visibility based on your account role (customer or admin). We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information, though no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

Data Retention

We retain your account and booking data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services, comply with legal obligations, and maintain business financial records. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us (see below).

Push Notifications

If you grant permission, we send push notifications about booking status changes and new chat messages. You can disable push notifications at any time through your device's notification settings.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us using the information below. We will respond to reasonable requests within a reasonable timeframe.

Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
